## Entry

**42 verified news events** across 6 continents, rendered as an international broadsheet newspaper (The Economist / Financial Times / NYT style). The entry is a 1024×1024 PNG (207 KB), generated deterministically via pure SVG → CairoSVG pipeline.

**Layout:** 3 lead story columns with detailed vector icons (48×48), emergency flashpoint strip (6 conflict zones), world map with numbered event pins, key figures box, 3 regional news panels (Americas, Europe & Africa, Asia-Pacific & Middle East), and a 20-item "Also On" wire index.

**Lead stories:** Xi-Trump-Putin APEC trilateral summit · ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya · GTA VI shatters all launch records ($2.3B day one).

**Sources:** Wikipedia (Current events portal, 19 Nov 2026), BBC News, Reuters, Associated Press (AP), Agence France-Presse (AFP), Google News — all 49 items occurred or were first reported on Thursday 19 November 2026 in any time zone.
